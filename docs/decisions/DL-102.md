## DL-102 — R1.4.4 fixes the live gameweek's labels and history, and speeds up CI's tests

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Decided by:** Owner, on request (the League and Home
reports during GW8, and the CI review's items 1 to 3) ·
**Serves:** FR-29, FR-32, NFR-09, NFR-15

### Context

GW8 was the first gameweek R1.4.3 ([DL-99](DL-99.md)) met, deployed after its matches had been
played. Two faults showed. The League table labelled the points of a team with no live figure
"GW7, last scored", but FPL's league table is read after the deadline and carries the gameweek in
play: the owner's 57 points that morning were GW8's (542 = 485 + 57). And Home's squad card showed
an empty "GW8 pts" column: one player, new mid-season, had his element summary fetched during GW8,
its partial GW8 row reached `player_gameweek`, and `history.json` counted eight gameweeks played.
The owner's own rows already kept finished gameweeks only (DL-69); the per-player rows did not.

Separately, a review of CI's test time found its two shards unequal: by measured time, one carries
341 test-seconds and the other 236, so the workflow waits on the heavier.

### Decision

1. **R1.4.4** is planned in [`R1.4.4-live-fixes-and-ci-speed.md`](../planning/epics/R1.4.4-live-fixes-and-ci-speed.md),
   on `dev/R1.4.4`, cut from `main` after R1.4.3's tag.
2. **`player_gameweek` keeps the rows of gameweeks FPL has finished**, as the owner's rows do. The
   live view reads `player_live`, so nothing loses a figure.
3. **A team with no live figure has its points labelled as the gameweek in play's, from FPL's
   table.** It is still listed after the live teams, with no gap to the leader: FPL updates its
   table on its own clock, not the scorer's.
4. CI's test shards are rebalanced and the slowest end-to-end tests trimmed, under their own
   decision once measured.

### Consequences

- A player first fetched mid-gameweek gets his row for it at the sweep after the check, like every
  other player (DL-93).
- The League table no longer claims a team is a gameweek behind; it says whose figure it shows.
