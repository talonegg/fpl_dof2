## DL-75 — Design principles narrowed to the ten that keep the codebase sound; five moved to editable guidance

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Decided by:** the owner asked for the principles to
keep only what must be immutable for an architecturally sound, maintainable, modular and extensible
codebase that agents can work in efficiently; an agent drafted the split and wording below.
Amends [DL-16](DL-16.md).

### Context

`docs/DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md` v1.0 held fifteen binding, amendment-controlled principles, read before
any design work: 27 KB. Five of them govern product presentation, modelling method and delivery
(DP-07, DP-08, DP-09, DP-10, DP-12). They are sound guidance, but they change as the product does, and
holding them behind the amendment hook made them waivers-or-defects rather than judgements. The file
also linked into documents archived by [DL-74](DL-74.md), kept alive by stubs, and claimed an
import-lint step for DP-01 that does not exist — `tests/test_source_isolation.py` is the check.

### Decision

1. **Binding (v2.0): DP-01 to DP-06, DP-11, DP-13, DP-14, DP-15** — source isolation, prediction and
   decision separated, pure core, typed contracts, rules and tunables in configuration,
   reproducibility, risk-led testing, traceability, graceful degradation.
2. **DP-14 widened** to "every artefact traces to its reason; every fact has one home", making the
   DL-74 documentation rules binding: code cites rather than narrates, a fact has one home, the home
   is updated in the same change. `python scripts/docs.py check` verifies it.
3. **Moved to [`docs/guiding-principles.md`](../guiding-principles.md), editable:** DP-07 vertical
   slices, DP-08 ships dark, DP-09 derivation/uncertainty/provenance, DP-10 arguable formulation,
   DP-12 baseline-relative skill. Departing from one is a decision, not a defect, and takes no
   `DP-WAIVER`. DP-08 now points to E8 §5 for the promotion bar instead of restating it.
4. **IDs are kept, never renumbered**, so the ~130 `DP-nn` citations in code still resolve.
5. Links re-pointed to `docs/decisions/` and the stubs left by DL-74 deleted; the amendment procedure
   now says how the hook is lifted; the stale review table and long provenance were cut.

### Consequences

- The binding file is about half its former size; the always-loaded pointer and CLAUDE.md list ten.
- Invariant 6 (variance on every forecast) still binds through CLAUDE.md and DP-02, although DP-09
  is now guidance.
- **Rejected:** renumbering the ten (breaks citations); scattering the five across E8, the design and
  the release register (each would then be restated in several homes).
