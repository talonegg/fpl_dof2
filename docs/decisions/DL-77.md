## DL-77 — The password gate is casual privacy, not a security boundary

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-17 (recording a change shipped 2026-10-14 in `8d61f95`) ·
**Decided by:** Owner · **Serves:** NFR-11, NFR-13

### Context

`web/src/gate/` added a client-side password gate: the entered password's SHA-256 is compared in the
browser with `VITE_FPL_PASSWORD_HASH`, it fails closed, and an unlock is remembered on the device. It
had no decision entry. The repository is public ([DL-12](DL-12.md)) and the published artefacts at
`/data/v1/*.json` are plain static files.

### Decision

1. The gate exists to keep **casual visitors** from browsing the app. It is **not** a security
   boundary and nothing may rely on it as one: the data it hides is publicly downloadable, and the
   bundle that checks the hash is public too.
2. Nothing secret or personal is ever published on the strength of the gate. What may be published is
   decided as if the gate did not exist (Invariant 10, NFR-13).
3. It is not authentication in the sense of NFR-11: no account, no FPL credential, no server.

### Consequences

- Real privacy would need different hosting and is out of scope at zero cost (Invariant 3).
- The hash, not the password, is the build input; it is set as described in
  [`environment.md`](../environment.md).
