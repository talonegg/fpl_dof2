## DL-78 — One planning horizon: the forecast's horizon reads the decision horizon

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Decided by:** Owner · **Serves:** FR-12, FR-18 ·
**Bears on:** Q-01, Q-02

### Context

Two horizons are configured. The forecast's `xp_horizon` sums **6** gameweeks at a discount of
**0.92** (`forecast.horizon_gameweeks`, `forecast.horizon_discount`); the multi-gameweek plan
optimises over **5** at **0.85** (`decision.horizon.gameweeks`, `decision.horizon.discount`). The app
shows `xp_horizon` beside a plan solved over a different window, so the number a reader sees is not
the number the plan maximised — two definitions of one tunable (DP-06).

### Decision

1. **`decision.horizon` is the single setting.** `xp_horizon` is computed over
   `decision.horizon.gameweeks` with `decision.horizon.discount`; the forecast-side settings are
   removed.
2. Q-01 (horizon length) and Q-02 (discount) remain open questions about that one setting.

### Consequences

- Not yet implemented: tracked as D-29 in the [debt register](../planning/debt-register.md) until the
  code change lands. Published `xp_horizon` values will change when it does (6 → 5 gameweeks,
  0.92 → 0.85), which the model card should note in the run that ships it.
