## DL-84 — The gameweek a run decides is the first deadline still ahead

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Decided by:** Agent, recording what PIPE-12 and
REM-08 built under [DL-79](DL-79.md) and [DL-81](DL-81.md) item 5, which named no definition ·
**Serves:** NFR-06, FR-37

### Context

The 2026-10-17 review found three definitions of "the next gameweek": the first unfinished, the
first deadline after now, and FPL's `is_next` (03 §12 P4, I-07). Between a deadline passing and that
gameweek finishing, they disagree. DL-79 made one definition PIPE-12's work without choosing it. The
build chose one in code, and the 2026-10-19 review listed the change among the decisions taken with
no entry. Training on a half-played gameweek was put back by DL-81 item 5.

### Decision

1. **The gameweek being decided is the first gameweek whose deadline is after the run's `as_of`**,
   leaving out any gameweek marked finished (`week.cadence.gameweek_ahead`). Forecast, week,
   decision, publish, transform and the day-before gate all read it. FPL's `is_next` is not used.
2. **The forecast's fit stops at the earlier of that gameweek's deadline and the first unfinished
   gameweek's deadline.** While GW *N* is in play, GW *N*+1 is decided and nothing from GW *N*
   trains the model.
3. **After the season's last deadline there is no gameweek ahead.** Stages that must name one
   (forecast, publish) use the final gameweek. A capture made then is knowable for no gameweek, so
   its `as_of_gameweek` is null ([DL-83](DL-83.md)).

### Consequences

- The published artefacts name one gameweek at any moment
  (`test_every_artefact_names_one_gameweek_while_a_gameweek_is_in_play`).
- During a gameweek in play, the forecast for GW *N*+1 knows nothing of GW *N*'s matches already
  played. That is accepted: the alternative trains on partial minutes and points.
