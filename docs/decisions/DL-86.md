## DL-86 — Downstream of the data pipeline, the platform becomes three modules around a strategy registry

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Decided by:** Owner, in a design interview ·
**Serves:** FR-04, FR-37, NFR-09, NFR-15

### Context

The owner wants three modules downstream of the data pipeline that can each be changed on its own:
an orchestrator that refreshes the gameweek, re-scores every model's advice as data arrives and
evaluates the finished gameweek; a model factory that tunes, trains and deploys models for
comparison; and the web layer. Many experiments are expected — on models, on the recommendation
policy and on the UI — and N models must be selectable and comparable. A review of the code on
2026-10-22 ([05 §2](../planning/05-target-architecture.md#2-where-the-code-stands--review-of-2026-10-22))
found three decision paths, model ids restated in six places, two of the five compared models living
only in a frozen bundle, a season log that scores only the champion, and a web build tied to every
data run.

### Decision

1. **Constraints unchanged.** Zero cost, no server, static Pages, Actions as the only production
   compute, public repository, no credentials.
2. **One repository, one release train, five logical modules:** data platform (unchanged, its
   silver and gold schemas the upstream contract), a pure `core` (rules, squad, the decision engine,
   one realised-points scorer), the orchestrator, the model factory and the web. Subpackages of
   `fpl_dof` with dependencies enforced by `import-linter` in CI; a physical split is deferred.
3. **The deployed unit is a strategy:** a forecaster paired with a decision policy, registered as
   committed YAML under `strategies/` with a stable id and a role; exactly one champion. The registry
   is the single source for the Python interface, the published registry artefact and each
   strategy's digest. Changing the champion is a role change plus a `DL-nn`.
4. **Forecasters are refit recipes by default**, recorded by digest; frozen fitted artefacts are a
   second type behind the same interface. Every forecaster emits a schema-validated forecast frame
   carrying variance; captaincy by price and position moves into the policy.
5. **The C1-versus-C3 study is retired.** `engine`, `c3`, `c1`, `a` and `a_odds` are ported as live
   strategies; the frozen harness runs one parity gameweek beside them and is then switched off,
   its records kept as history.
6. **Every published strategy gets the full recommendation** from the one engine, with a
   per-strategy `plan: false` switch, and **all are refreshed together on one input snapshot**
   whenever a material change (prices, status, odds, fixtures) or the scheduled tick calls for a
   run. Ingest dispatches the orchestrator through typed inputs; nothing chains by workflow name.
7. **The advice of record is the last successful recommendation before the deadline**, per
   strategy, fail-closed as "no advice". Revision history is backlog.
8. **Evaluation runs once per finished gameweek**, from the owner's real squad: xP accuracy, advice
   quality against what was played and against a hindsight optimum on real prices, and
   reconciliation. Results are kept on `ledger` under `evaluation/`; the season log is built from
   them.
9. **Experiments are declared files** (hypothesis, falsifier, baseline, variant, metric, length),
   their results stamped with the strategy digest and kept on `ledger` under `experiments/`. The
   weekly backtest becomes a standing experiment over every strategy. Nothing promotes itself;
   E8 §5 stays the bar mid-season.
10. **The web renders registries**, compares any N strategies, takes UI variants from owner-toggled
    flags, and is released independently: app built once per web change, data deploys repack it,
    contract major checked at deploy and at runtime, additive changes in `v1` with `contract_minor`,
    types generated in the web build.
11. **Delivery:** R1.2.0 (registry and five models live), R1.3.0 (gameweek orchestration), R1.4.0
    (independent web). Blocking findings from 03 §12 and §12.6 are folded into the slice that
    touches them; F1 and F3 become the first registered experiments.

### Consequences

- The design is [`05-target-architecture.md`](../planning/05-target-architecture.md), living; each
  release moves what it builds into 03. Stories: the
  [R1.2.0 backlog](../planning/epics/R1.2.0-modular-platform-backlog.md).
- Retiring the pre-registered study ends its primary test unreported at full length; its records
  stay on `ledger` and the app keeps showing them.
- Refreshing every strategy together costs CI minutes in proportion to N; free on a public
  repository, controlled by `plan: false` and by which strategies are published.
