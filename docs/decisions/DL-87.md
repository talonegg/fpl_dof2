## DL-87 — R1.2.0 ships in two drops; the archive and football-data.co.uk run live; no parity gameweek

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Decided by:** Owner, in a review of the R1.2.0
backlog · **Serves:** FR-04, FR-37, NFR-06, NFR-15

Superseded in part by [DL-88](DL-88.md): the second drop, R1.2.1 below, is renumbered R1.2.2.

### Context

The R1.2.0 backlog ([DL-86](DL-86.md)) put all nine stories behind one promotion, with the
boundaries refactor (MOD-01) first on a serial critical path and a parity gameweek (MOD-06) that
could only run on `main`. A and A_odds, two of the five models to be ported, train on prior seasons
from the FPL archive, and A_odds also reads historical and upcoming football-data.co.uk odds; the
live `run` job enables neither source. The frozen harness records each model's decisions and xP but
only hashes of its inputs, so an exact offline replay is not possible.

### Decision

1. **Two drops.** R1.2.0: the registry slice — MOD-03, MOD-02, MOD-04, with MOD-07 built alongside
   and MOD-01 started. R1.2.1: the data change below, MOD-05, MOD-08, MOD-09 and MOD-06. Promotion
   timing is unrestricted.
2. **MOD-01 is a ratchet.** `import-linter` goes in first with an allow-list of today's violations
   that only shrinks as stories touch the code; it is empty before R1.2.1 promotes.
3. **The registry** lives in `pipeline/src/fpl_dof/config/strategies/*.yaml`. Ids are `c3`
   (champion), `c1`, `engine`, `a` and `a_odds` (challengers); the web's old `champion` and
   `challenger` values in `?model=` and saved preferences are mapped once to the new ids.
4. **The comparison UI**: the existing switcher lists every published strategy and applies on every
   page; `/models` gains a multi-select comparison — advice as differences from the champion, xP
   where they disagree, historical performance — with every published strategy selected by default.
5. **`fplarchive` and `fdcouk` are enabled in the live `run` job.** `fdcouk` captures are marked
   immutable, so each run's odds are kept on `data` whatever their age, as well as in the gameweek
   tarball on `snapshots`. A_odds records "no advice" when its odds are missing.
6. **No parity check.** This supersedes DL-86 item 5's parity gameweek. The promotion that publishes
   the ported strategies switches the frozen harness off; each strategy's history shows the frozen
   records up to that gameweek and live records after, with the switch marked.

### Consequences

- R1.2.0 reaches production sooner and smaller; the boundaries refactor no longer gates it.
- A port that differs from its frozen original is not detected by a check: a change in a strategy's
  results at the switch may be the model or the port, and the marker is the only signal.
- The live run restores the archive's immutable bronze and fetches odds every run; both are free
  and already retained by PIPE-03 and PIPE-14.
