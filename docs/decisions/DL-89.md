## DL-89 — R1.2.2: the switch when it promotes, F1 as a bug fix, live history scored on the real squad

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Decided by:** Owner, at the start of the R1.2.2 build
· **Serves:** FR-04, FR-37, NFR-06, NFR-15

### Context

R1.2.2 ([DL-87](DL-87.md), renumbered by [DL-88](DL-88.md)) ports the five strategies, turns the
archive and football-data.co.uk on in the live run, and retires the frozen harness. The build survey
found three questions DL-87 left open and three ways that turning the archive on would silently
change live output:

- The champion trains on every season present, so prior seasons would change its training window.
  That is the F3 experiment, taken without a decision.
- The purchase-price index keys on gameweek and player id, which FPL reuses across seasons.
- `immutable` on a bronze resource also means "never re-fetch", so marking odds captures immutable
  would freeze them at their first capture.

### Decision

1. **The switch is whenever R1.2.2 promotes.** The switch gameweek is the first gameweek the frozen
   harness has not decided when the promotion lands. There is no target gameweek.
2. **F1 (M7's card rate) is a bug fix** under E8 §5's exception. It ships into the champion in
   R1.2.2, after the backtest has graded it. If the backtest shows it degrading the held-out season,
   it stops and goes back to the owner. **F3 (the training window) stays a model change** under
   E8 §5. It is graded in the standing backtest, and its outcome (promoted, rejected or extended) is
   recorded in its own DL entry.
3. **Live history after the switch is each strategy's advice on the owner's real squad**, scored per
   gameweek by `week/score_advice.py` until ORC-04 replaces the scorer. The frozen records are each
   model's virtual season. The switch marker is the only thing separating the two.
4. **Training window is a declared field.** `training_window` is `current_season` or
   `all_seasons`. `c3` and `engine` keep `current_season`, so the champion is unchanged by the
   archive. `c1`, `a` and `a_odds` take `all_seasons`, as their frozen originals did.
5. **Kept is not immutable.** A separate `retain` flag keeps every `fdcouk` capture on `data`
   whatever its age. `immutable` keeps its meaning of "never re-fetch".
6. **Prior seasons never reach current-season outputs.** The price index, the season log and any
   other single-season consumer filter to the current season.
7. **A and A_odds are ported, not copied.** Scoring comes from the rules config, tunables from config,
   and odds from the silver `team_match_expectation` the `fdcouk` adapter writes. They do not use the
   frozen bundle's Pinnacle and Shin de-vig reading. The differences are listed in the port's
   docstring and in 05 §5. As DL-87 says, nothing checks them against the frozen numbers.

### Consequences

- Turning the archive on changes no champion output. The golden-artefact test
  (`pipeline/tests/test_golden_artefacts.py`) shows it.
- The champion's numbers move in R1.2.2 only through F1, which is graded first and named as a bug
  fix.
- The history chart joins two quantities with different meanings: frozen virtual seasons and
  real-squad advice. Read across the switch with that in mind.
