## DL-89 — football-data.co.uk captures are retained by a flag of their own, not marked immutable

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Decided by:** Owner, on request · **Serves:** NFR-06, FR-04

### Context

[DL-87](DL-87.md) item 5 said `fdcouk` captures would be marked immutable so every run's odds stay on
`data`. In `sources/fetch.py` immutable also means a cached snapshot is never re-fetched, which
would freeze the upcoming-odds file at its first capture while the prices move.

### Decision

1. A resource may declare `retain`; the flag is written to each snapshot's sidecar and
   `pipeline/scripts/retention.py` keeps every such snapshot whatever its age. It does not affect
   caching: a retained resource is re-fetched after its TTL.
2. `fdcouk`'s upcoming-odds resource declares `retain`. Its completed-season files stay immutable.
3. Past-season odds are stamped with football-data's documented pre-match collection time (Friday
   18:00 London for weekend games, Tuesday 18:00 for midweek, never after kickoff), not with
   kickoff, so a backtest never reads them as taken after the deadline.

### Consequences

- This supersedes DL-87 item 5's word "immutable" and nothing else in it.
- The `data` branch grows by one small CSV per fixtures fetch; the budget is the `data` branch's,
  not a paid service's.
