## DL-91 — F3 is rejected: the champion keeps its current-season window; both experiments retire

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Decided by:** Owner, on the MOD-09 results ·
**Serves:** FR-04, FR-37, NFR-06

Supersedes in part [DL-90](DL-90.md): its decision item 1 says `c3_f1` was retired when F1
shipped. It stayed an experiment until the CI backtest had recorded F1, as described below.

### Context

[DL-89](DL-89.md) item 2 kept F3 a model change under
[E8 §5](../planning/epics/E8-ongoing-in-season-monitoring.md#5-the-bar-for-changing-the-model-mid-season),
with the owner to decide. The variant `c3_f3` is the champion trained on every season silver holds
instead of the current season only. It was graded on the walk-forward over 2024/25 and 2025/26
(68 folds, 20,573 scored rows), paired against `c3` per gameweek, with a seeded 95% bootstrap
interval:

| Metric | `c3_f3` minus `c3` | Better is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MAE | +0.01189 [+0.00712, +0.01665] | lower |
| Spearman | −0.01736 [−0.02466, −0.01006] | higher |
| Top-N precision | −0.00221 [−0.01176, +0.00588] | higher |

Pooled MAE was 1.9175 against 1.9056 and calibration slope 0.718 against 0.758. All-seasons
training is significantly worse on the held-out seasons, so E8 §5 condition 1 fails. The CI
backtest's record, `ledger:experiments/20261023T025830Z-e93622d9.json`, reproduces it: MAE +0.01199 [+0.00725, +0.01670], Spearman
−0.01680 [−0.02406, −0.00954], top-N precision 0.00000 [−0.01103, +0.01029].

To put F1's result on `ledger` after F1 shipped, `c3_f1` was redefined as the pre-fix control:
the champion without `yellow_cards` in its rolling statistics, graded against the fixed `c3` by the
CI backtest. The same record has the control minus `c3` at MAE −0.00052 [−0.00121, +0.00021],
Spearman −0.00002 [−0.00094, +0.00089] and top-N precision −0.00147 [−0.00735, +0.00368]: none
significant, the mirror of DL-90's local run.

### Decision

1. **F3 is rejected.** `c3` keeps `training_window: current_season`. The declared experiment
   `f3_training_window` records `decision: rejected`.
2. **`c3_f3` and `c3_f1` retire** once the CI backtest has written their records to
   `ledger:experiments/`. Their registry entries stay, with `role: retired`, so the declared
   experiments' variants remain registered. The standing backtest no longer grades them.

### Consequences

- The champion's training window is unchanged; F3's mismatch between what is graded and what ships
  is closed by MOD-08's grading in each strategy's own window, not by widening the window.
- A later case for more training data needs a new experiment: for example, prior seasons weighted
  down rather than pooled, or used only early in a season.
