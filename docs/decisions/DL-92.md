## DL-92 — Hotfix R1.2.3: a purchase price is what was paid; an unknown one never overstates the budget

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Decided by:** Owner, on request ·
**Serves:** FR-09, FR-25

### Context

The squad state priced every held player at his price in the latest picks gameweek, not at what
was paid. In CI those prices come from element summaries, which the `data` branch refreshes in full
only weekly. From 2026-10-21 the picks ran to GW7 while the owner's players' gameweek rows stopped at
GW6. Every lookup missed and fell back to the current price. The GW8 advice then carried 15 "no
purchase price" warnings and a sell value of £100.7m against £99.0m. The overstated budget could
recommend transfers the owner cannot afford. The release procedure tags a hotfix with the patch
number after `main`'s latest tag, R1.2.2.

### Decision

1. **The hotfix is R1.2.3.**
2. **A purchase price is what was paid** (`squad/state.py`). In order:
   - the picks row's own price, where the source gives one;
   - else the cost of the latest transfer that brought the player in;
   - else his price in the first gameweek he was held.
3. **When none is known, the lowest price seen this season, with a warning.** It can understate the
   selling price but never overstate it, so the advice never spends money the owner does not have.
   The old fallback, the current price, overstated it for every riser.

### Consequences

- Sell values fall back to the selling-price rule (half of any rise, rounded down) wherever the
  purchase price is known. The champion's transfers on the current data are unchanged; only the
  budget and the warnings move.
- A player bought during a Free Hit is not treated specially. His squad reverts after the Free Hit,
  so the transfer log's "latest in" may name a Free Hit purchase. That case is left until it is
  seen.
