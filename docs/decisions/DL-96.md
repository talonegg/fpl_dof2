## DL-96 — The re-rank's percentile is taken without the horizon discount

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Decided by:** Owner, on request ·
**Serves:** FR-20, FR-21

### Context

The simulation re-rank pulled chips to the first horizon gameweek (D-21, [DL-28](DL-28.md)).
EXP-02's declared experiment (`d21_chip_timing`) found the cause: `plan._draws` applied the
per-gameweek horizon discount to each week's draws before the dial's percentile was taken, which
shrinks a later week's upside tail as well as its mean. Over eight replayed deadlines the first chip
landed on the first horizon gameweek at 1 of 8 with the re-rank off, 4 of 8 as shipped and 0–1 of 8
with the percentile undiscounted, at every dial; mean chip-week points were 78.9, 70.6 and
78.9–82.8. Chip set 1 needs a plan by GW15 and expires at the GW19 deadline.

### Decision

1. **`decision.simulation.undiscounted_percentile` is on by default.** The discount stays in the
   MILP's objective, as a time preference over means, and is kept out of the re-rank's percentile.
2. **It is treated as a correction, not a model change awaiting six shadow gameweeks** (E8 §5).
   The mechanism was explained before it was measured and the evidence is from held-out seasons;
   a chip set is played once, so live shadow accuracy cannot be gathered before it expires.

### Consequences

- Chip timing in the published plan and chip calendar changes from the run that ships it.
- The evidence is eight deadlines: it shows the front-loading is gone, not that chips are timed
  better. The weekly backtest's chip-timing replay keeps re-measuring it (`config/ci-backtest.yaml`).
- D-21 is closed.
