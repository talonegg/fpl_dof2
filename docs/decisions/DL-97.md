## DL-97 — "As if played" is today's models replayed in-sample on the season's past gameweeks

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Decided by:** Owner, on request ·
**Serves:** FR-41, FR-42 · **Supersedes:** [DL-95](DL-95.md) item 4

### Context

DL-95 item 4 had the live season replayed walk-forward: each model refitted at every deadline on
what was knowable then. ORC-13 built that and found it could start only at GW5 (before then the
live models published their cold-start fallback) and could play no chips (the walk produces no
multi-gameweek forecast). The owner's intent is different: how each model *as it stands today*
would have played the season so far.

### Decision

1. **"As if played" replays each model with its latest fitted weights** at every finished gameweek
   from GW1, on its own squad: a from-scratch GW1 squad under the budget, then the full decision
   engine at each deadline (transfers, chips and hits), scored by the one realised scorer.
2. **Inputs carry no look-ahead**: features, prices, availability and fixtures are those knowable at
   each deadline (Invariant 5). Only the weights are later than the gameweek they play.
3. **It is in-sample and says so**: the app labels it "In-sample: today's models replayed on past
   gameweeks; not evidence of skill". It never feeds the champion, a promotion or the E8 §5 bar;
   the out-of-sample record stays with the live ladder of each model's advice on the owner's squad
   and the 2025/26 replay.
4. **The walk-forward live-season replay is removed**, not kept beside it.
5. A model that cannot be run at a past deadline (its inputs absent then) is shown as unavailable for
   that gameweek, with the reason.

### Consequences

- The ladder flatters every model, most of all one that fits this season's noise; the label is the
  only guard, so it is shown wherever the ladder is.
- Chips and GW1–4 are covered, which the walk-forward replay could not do.
- Its run time is measured before it is placed in the weekly backtest or a job of its own.
