## DL-98 — E2 runs live in shadow, one gameweek at a time, to earn its six shadow gameweeks

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Decided by:** Owner, on the agent's recommendation ·
**Serves:** FR-41, NFR-14

### Context

`e2_rating_prior` (D-34) shrinks M2's club ratings towards the league-neutral 1.0. The standing
backtest graded it on 2026-10-24 (run `20261024T045556Z-d645e97e`, 68 held-out folds): MAE better
than c3 with an interval that excludes zero, Spearman unchanged. That is condition 1 of the
[E8 §5 bar](../planning/epics/E8-ongoing-in-season-monitoring.md#5-the-bar-for-changing-the-model-mid-season).
Condition 2, six live shadow gameweeks without degrading, cannot start while `c3_e2` is unpublished:
it never runs live, so the app cannot select it and its prospective accuracy is never recorded.

Publishing it as graded, with c3's multi-gameweek plan, does not fit the pipeline's 300 s budget
(ORC-09): on the runner c3's plan took 110 s of a 240 s run, and the week stage advises each model in
turn, so a second plan would make a run about 350 s.

### Decision

1. **`c3_e2` is published**: it runs every refresh, appears in the model selector as an experiment,
   and records its predictions and advice like any published model. It is never the live advice;
   its role stays `experiment`.
2. **It advises one gameweek at a time, without chips** (`plan: false`), as `c1` and `a` do. The
   forecast, which is what E2 changes and what condition 2 measures, is unchanged.
3. **Its six shadow gameweeks count from its first published gameweek.** Promotion still needs all
   three E8 §5 conditions and an owner decision in a new entry.

### Consequences

- The pipeline gains one forecast and one single-gameweek solve, about 10–15 s on the runner.
- Its live plan and chip advice, its 2025/26 playthrough and its as-if-played ladder all play one
  gameweek at a time, so they do not compare like for like with c3's; its forecast accuracy does.
- If it is promoted, its plan is c3's: the plan policy is not what was tested.
