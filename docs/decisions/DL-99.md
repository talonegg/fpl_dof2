## DL-99 — R1.4.3 refreshes the app after each matchday and at gameweek completion, with provisional points and a live league table

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Decided by:** Owner, on request; the agent's design
and its four recommended answers (the plan's §5) accepted the same day ·
**Serves:** FR-26, FR-32, FR-35, NFR-01, NFR-15

### Context

A gameweek's points reach the app only once FPL marks it finished and data-checked, and only at the
next run. The ingest fetches live scores, picks and entry history for finished gameweeks alone, and
no change during a gameweek starts a run: the change manifest's kinds are prices, status, fixtures
and odds (DL-94 item 4), and the pipeline's scheduled tick runs only in the Open gameweek's
pre-deadline windows. Over a gameweek weekend the owner's points, the rivals' and the mini-league
table are a gameweek old.

### Decision

1. **R1.4.3, the live gameweek**, is planned in
   [`R1.4.3-live-gameweek.md`](../planning/epics/R1.4.3-live-gameweek.md): LIVE-01 to LIVE-07, one
   drop, on `dev/R1.4.3`.
2. **The app refreshes once per matchday and once at completion.** A matchday is the fixtures that
   share a kick-off date in UK time. It is done when its last fixture's bonus is confirmed, or at a
   settle cap after its last kick-off, published as "bonus pending"; bonus is never estimated from
   BPS. A new change kind, `results`, moves then, when the gameweek is marked finished, and when it
   is data-checked; each starts one full pipeline run that deploys, not a live-only path. Nothing runs during a match.
3. **Provisional points come from the one realised scorer** (ORC-04), extended for a gameweek in
   play: a starter is substituted only once all his fixtures are finished. Each published team's
   provisional points and live total are published in a new artefact, `live.json`, and the League
   table and Home read it. A warning gate, not a blocking one, checks the scorer against FPL's
   own figures once the gameweek is finished.
4. **Provisional figures are never the record.** The season log and the evaluation wait for
   data-checked, as now (ORC-05), and provisional rows never become features (Invariant 5).
5. **The R-09 freeze binds the new runs**; the guard admits a `results` run when no gameweek is
   Open, so the season's last weekend refreshes too.

### Consequences

- The fast ingest runs hourly while a gameweek is Playing: two requests an hour. A typical weekend
  adds four to six pipeline runs, free on a public repository (NFR-01).
- Picks for the gameweek in play cost one request per published team, once, after the deadline.
- A team past `league_rival_limit` has no live figure; the table shows FPL's own for it.
- The scorer gains in-play rules, so the realised conformance corpus and the 100% rules coverage
  gate carry them.
- A matchday run also re-forecasts and re-advises the next gameweek, which may change its advice
  before the deadline, as any run may.
