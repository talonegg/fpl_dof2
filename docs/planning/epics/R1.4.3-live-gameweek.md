# R1.4.3 Live Gameweek

**Derived From**: the owner's request of 2026-10-26; decided by [DL-99](../../decisions/DL-99.md)
**Status**: Open on `dev/R1.4.3`, 2026-10-26. Not started; the owner's answers are in §5
**Target Release**: R1.4.3
**Created**: 2026-10-26

---

## 1. Overview

Today the app learns a gameweek's points only when FPL marks it finished and data-checked, and then
only at the next run: the ingest fetches live scores, picks and entry history for finished
gameweeks alone (`sources/game/adapter.py`), and nothing starts a run when a result comes in. All
weekend the owner's points, the rivals' points and the league table are last gameweek's.

This release refreshes the app **once at the end of each matchday** of the gameweek being played
(after the day's last match and its bonus) and **once when the gameweek completes**, recomputing
every published team's points and the mini-league table each time. The figures are provisional
until FPL data-checks the gameweek, and say so; the season log and the evaluation remain the record
and are untouched until then (ORC-05).

```
┌──────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Item     │ Title                                                         │
├──────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LIVE-01  │ The gameweek being played, ingested                           │
│ LIVE-02  │ Matchday and completion refreshes                             │
│ LIVE-03  │ Provisional points from the one scorer                        │
│ LIVE-04  │ `live.json`: the gameweek in play, published                  │
│ LIVE-05  │ The live league table                                         │
│ LIVE-06  │ Home and the squad during the gameweek                        │
│ LIVE-07  │ Gameweek completion: final points and the evaluation at once  │
└──────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Order.** LIVE-03 first: it is the rule that, wrong, is invisible (DP-13), and everything else
publishes it. Then LIVE-01 and LIVE-04 together, LIVE-02, and the views (LIVE-05, LIVE-06) last.
LIVE-07 closes it. One drop; every contract change is an additive minor. It is promoted as soon
as the local checks pass, never inside a deadline's last 24 hours, and verified on the first
gameweek weekend after (§5 Q7).

**Terms.** A gameweek is *Playing* from its first kick-off (ORC-01). A **matchday** is the
Playing gameweek's fixtures that share a kick-off date in UK time (§5 Q1). A matchday is
**done** when every one of its fixtures is finished with bonus confirmed (FPL's fixture `finished`),
or when the settle cap has passed since its last kick-off (§5 Q2).

## 2. Stories

### LIVE-01: The gameweek being played, ingested

#### Design
The **live gameweek** is the latest past its deadline: **in play** until FPL data-checks it, then
final until the next deadline.

1. Past its deadline, the gameweek's picks are fixed. The ingest fetches the owner's and each
   published rival's picks for it **once** after the deadline (up to `league_rival_limit`, as
   ORC-14), kept in bronze by entry and gameweek, so a matchday costs no picks requests; and once
   more when it is data-checked, because the picks carry FPL's own `entry_history`, final only then.
   The fetcher serves a capture taken from a stated moment (`fresh_from`) whatever its age.
2. The pipeline run never ingests; the **fast ingest** does. On every firing while a gameweek is in
   play it also fetches `event/{gw}/live/` for it and the league standings (if a league is
   configured), as fresh as the fixtures capture read with them: one or two requests. Once the
   gameweek is checked, each is fetched once more, for the final figures, and then never.
3. Silver gains **`player_live`**: per player and fixture of his club's in the live gameweek,
   minutes, points, BPS, bonus, `finished` (full time, FPL's `finished_provisional`) and
   `bonus_confirmed` (FPL's `finished`), stamped `observed_at` and `provisional`. A fixture still to
   kick off, or one a player has no line in, is a row of zeros, so a player yet to play is never
   read as a blank. Its Pandera schema holds it to one gameweek; the transform refuses a row whose
   gameweek was not past its deadline when it was observed or at the run, because the deadline is
   in the gameweek table. **`entry_live`** carries each fetched team's own figures from its picks
   (points, hits, total, chip), for LIVE-03's check; the season log's `entry_gameweek` still holds
   finished gameweeks only. The in-play picks join `entry_pick` and `league_pick`, keyed by
   gameweek. So the squad state reads the chip in force from their own active chip
   (`entry_live.chip`) as well as from entry history, which can predate the deadline
   (`gameweek.chips_played`): a Free Hit in play is never the squad next gameweek's advice
   starts from (D-33). The fixture table gains `started` and `finished_provisional`.
4. **Provisional rows are never features.** The feature store reads `player_gameweek` only, as now;
   it refuses a frame stamped `provisional` with a `LeakageError`, the look-ahead check (PIPE-10),
   and no strategy may require a live table (Invariant 5).
5. Only `sources/` knows where these come from (Invariant 1); FPL's public endpoints, no login
   (Invariant 4).

### LIVE-02: Matchday and completion refreshes

#### Design
1. **A new change kind, `results`** ([DL-94](../../decisions/DL-94.md) item 4's manifest):
   the fast ingest's fixtures capture moves it when a matchday becomes done, and its
   bootstrap-static capture when the gameweek is marked finished and when it is marked
   data-checked. Nothing else moves it, so one matchday starts one run, and a kick-off or a goal
   starts none. The rule is pure and source-free (`gameweek.py` `matchdays_done_since`); it
   compares the restored capture with the new one at each capture's `fetched_at`, so a recapture of
   the same bytes past the settle cap still moves it, once. The kind carries no payload: the run
   reads "bonus pending" from the same fixture flags.
2. **The fast ingest runs hourly while a gameweek is in play**, from its deadline until it is
   data-checked (`gameweek.py` `gameweeks_in_play`), not four-hourly, so a done matchday is seen
   within the hour. Two cheap requests an hour (NFR-01). Their captures expire after 50 minutes
   (`_FAST_PATH_TTL`, inside the hour), so each firing refetches rather than reading the last one.
3. **The pipeline guard lets a `change:results` run go ahead** for the Playing gameweek, and when no
   gameweek is Open (the season's last weekend). The R-09 freeze still binds it.
4. Each such run is a full pipeline run (§5 Q4); its last job deploys, as every run that published
   does (ORC-03). The run banner names it a "results refresh", in the lower case of its siblings
   ("day-before refresh").
5. New tunables, named, defaulted and justified in `ScheduleConfig` (DP-06): the matchday zone
   (`matchday_zone`, `Europe/London`) and the settle cap (`matchday_settle_hours`, 3).

### LIVE-03: Provisional points from the one scorer

#### Design
1. **One scorer still** (ORC-04): `rules/realised.py` `score_lineup` gains what a gameweek in play
   needs, and nothing gets a second copy. A player's gameweek is complete once every fixture he has
   is finished, at full time (or at once if he has none); a performance carries it, and
   `performances` reads it from a per-fixture `finished` column, which a gameweek in play gives a
   row for every fixture, one yet to kick off included. A starter is substituted only once his
   gameweek is complete with no minutes; a starter yet to play keeps his place and scores what he
   has so far, and a substitute yet to play cannot come on. The captain's armband passes to the
   vice-captain on the same rule. A finished gameweek, every performance complete, scores as before.
2. Each published team's **provisional gameweek points** are that score less its hits; its **live
   total** is its total after the gameweek before plus them (`live_score`: the hit is the
   gameweek's own, taken once). That total is the game's, from the team's per-gameweek history:
   the owner's `entry_gameweek`, a rival's `league_gameweek` (which carries `total_points` per
   finished gameweek), and 0 before GW1 (`live.py` `last_total`). Not FPL's total in the picks
   capture less the gameweek: whether that total already counts the gameweek's points, or its
   hit, depends on the moment of capture, and the in-play picks are captured once, just after
   the deadline. A team with no such row has no live figure and says why; its gameweek points
   are still checked against FPL's (item 4).
3. Each team carries a status (`gameweek.team_status`, over the fixtures its players have):
   `in_play` (a fixture to come), `bonus_pending` (all played, a bonus not confirmed), `complete`
   (finished, not data-checked) or `final`: data-checked, and the live scores and the team's own
   figures are the captures taken after the check. If a refetch after the check failed, the team
   (and, for the scores, the gameweek) stays `complete`, provisional.
4. **A quality gate checks the scorer against FPL** once the gameweek is finished and FPL's own
   figures for it are final: each fetched team's gameweek points against the game's, both net of
   the hit, from the data-checked capture of its picks (before that they are the deadline's,
   LIVE-01). A warning, not a block, with no tolerance (§5 Q3). **Known limit:** the check runs
   only when FPL data-checks the gameweek before the next deadline. A gameweek checked after it
   (a Monday match, a Tuesday deadline) has already left the live tables, which hold the next
   gameweek by then, so its scorer check is lost; its evaluation is not (LIVE-07).

### LIVE-04: `live.json`: the gameweek in play, published

   deadline, when the live tables move on to the next gameweek (LIVE-01); between that deadline
   and its first kick-off there is none. It holds the gameweek, its status, `as_of`, the matchdays
   done and to come, each fixture's state, and for the owner and each team in the league table
   their points, hits, chip, live total, rank among the published teams, FPL's own figures and
   whether the scorer matches them, and per player minutes, points, bonus confirmed or pending,
   the multiplier as applied and the projected substitutions. A team without picks, FPL's figures

2. **Absent, not empty**, preseason and whenever no gameweek has kicked off (DP-15), as

3. **One home** (DP-14): `live.json` is the provisional view and says so; `log.json` and the

### LIVE-05: The live league table

#### Design
1. League shows the table by live total while a gameweek is in play: live rank, the movement since
   FPL's last rank (that rank less the live rank), each team's gameweek points and status,
   "Provisional" until final, and a visible note where the scorer differs from FPL
   (`matches_game` false).
2. A team past `league_rival_limit` has no picks to score; it shows the league table's own
   figures, marked "not live", and is ranked by them. Its total is a gameweek behind the live

### LIVE-06: Home and the squad during the gameweek

#### Design
Home's squad card, while a gameweek is in play, shows each player's points so far, the captain's
multiplied, the projected substitutions, the gameweek total, its status and the time of the last
refresh. The next gameweek's xP and each model's advice stay beside it.

An app left open re-reads the published files when it becomes visible again and updates in place,
so a matchday refresh is seen without a reload; nothing is fetched while it is hidden, and there is
no timer (§5 Q5). Data stays `NetworkFirst`, as now.

The re-read is `DataStore.refresh()`: it drops the cached files and bumps a generation the views
subscribe to (`useArtefact`, `DataProvider`), so they read again and keep what they show until the
new files arrive, or for good if the read fails. A `visibilitychange` to visible is its only
trigger (`data/refresh.ts`).

### LIVE-07: Gameweek completion: final points and the evaluation at once

#### Design
The `results` change for data-checked starts the run in which the gameweek turns Finished (ORC-01),
so its evaluation (ORC-05), the season log and Last gameweek update in that run, not at the next
daily refresh. `live.json` reads `final` until the next deadline moves the live tables on (LIVE-04).

The evaluation reads `player_gameweek`, which comes from the per-player summaries, and a summary
fetched before the check lacks the checked gameweek's final rows; the slow ingest would refresh them
only at its next daily sweep. So the fast ingest whose capture is the first to show a gameweek
data-checked fetches the summaries that predate the check, once (`on_check`, ~700 requests, about
six minutes), and the run it starts evaluates on the final rows. It keys on the gameweek checked,
not the live one (`adapter.py` `_newly_checked`): a gameweek FPL checks after the next deadline is
no longer the live gameweek, and is swept and evaluated the same way. No other fast firing
fetches or walks them: each reads the bootstrap capture before its own and sees the check is not
new. The evaluation itself cannot tell a pre-check row from a final one, since silver's
`player_gameweek` carries no capture time and the gameweek table no check time; the sweep is
what keeps it on the final rows.

#### Acceptance criteria
- [x] A gameweek marked data-checked is evaluated by the run its change started; a test drives the

  `test_a_data_checked_gameweek_is_evaluated_by_the_run_its_change_started` drives one gameweek
  through the real adapter, transform and stages: the run before the check (finished, every row in)

## 3. Out of scope

- **In-play refresh**, minute by minute. A matchday's end is the unit; nothing runs during a match.
- **Provisional bonus from BPS** while a match's bonus is unconfirmed (§5 Q2).
- **The forecast refitted on the weekend's results.** A matchday run re-forecasts on what silver
  holds; `player_gameweek` still fills from the slow ingest, as now.
- **A live table for every entry** of a large league: the picks budget stays `league_rival_limit`.
- **Push notifications.** The app shows the refresh; nothing is sent.

## 4. Risks

| Risk | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| GitHub drops or delays the fast ingest's hourly firing (D-35) | The next firing sees the same done matchday; the manifest moves once, whichever firing sees it |
| FPL confirms bonus late, or corrects points after a matchday | The settle cap publishes with bonus pending; the next `results` change, and data-checked at the latest, corrects it |
| A matchday run overlaps a pre-deadline run (midweek gameweeks) | Runs queue in the `run` job's concurrency group; the R-09 freeze binds both |
